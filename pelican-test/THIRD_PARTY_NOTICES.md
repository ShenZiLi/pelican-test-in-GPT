All film scenes, rig, score, and procedural assets are independently authored for The Pelican Test. No code, briefs, film frames, cover, samples, or soundtrack from Tunai-0511/meme67-art-history are incorporated. That repository was read only for research.

## Direct components

p5.js is unmodified LGPL-2.1 library code, kept as a separate dependency. Its source is available in the installed p5 package and https://github.com/processing/p5.js; the exact version is recorded below and in package-lock.json. It can be replaced independently by editing the p5 script path in index.html. p5.brush is MIT. Noto Serif SC is distributed under the SIL Open Font License; its license is retained. Browser and ffmpeg executables are local tools and are not included in the source delivery. ffmpeg build details are recorded in the final acceptance report.
