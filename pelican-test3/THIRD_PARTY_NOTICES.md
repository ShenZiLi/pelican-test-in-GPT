## Historical art references

The historical sections evoke materials and techniques of Tassili rock painting, ancient Egyptian murals, Han rubbings, Tang line painting, Song landscapes, Chinese urban handscrolls, Maya codices, Mughal miniatures, Bada Shanren ink, Wayang Kulit, Hiroshige-era prints, Van Gogh-era impasto and Munch-era expressive line. They are new compositions, not reproductions of digitized museum photographs. The silent-film section is an original staircase and pelican shadow, not a copied film frame. The marble observer is original procedural geometry.

FeatherLink, Pedal Quest and LOOP are fictional interfaces created here. No real app logos, game characters, levels, album covers, samples or notification sounds are included. No Kokoro voice samples or other external audio is used.
